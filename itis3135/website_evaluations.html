Yasmine's ITIS 3135

Website Evaluations

Apple

  • Contrast: Apple does a good job using contrast to make important information stand out. The large images, bold headings, and simple color choices make the main content easy to notice.
  • Repetition: The website consistently repeats its typography, spacing, buttons, and overall visual style throughout the different pages. This makes the site feel consistent and connected.
  • Alignment: Apple's content is carefully aligned and spaced. Text, images, buttons, and sections have a clear structure instead of looking randomly placed.
  • Proximity: Related information is grouped together. Product images, descriptions, and buttons are placed close to each other, making it easier to understand what information belongs together.

Arngren

  • Contrast: The website has poor contrast because there are many different colors, font sizes, and visual elements competing for attention. It is difficult to tell what the most important information is.
  • Repetition: There is very little consistent visual repetition. Products, text, images, and links have different styles and sizes, which makes the page feel unorganized.
  • Alignment: The page has weak alignment because many products and pieces of text appear scattered across the page. There is not a clear grid or structure guiding the user's eyes.
  • Proximity: Related information is not always grouped clearly. Images, prices, descriptions, and links can be spread out, making it harder to figure out which information belongs to each product.
